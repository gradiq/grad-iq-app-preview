About Grad-IQ Planners

Four planners for graduate students: PhD Stipend & Loan Budget, Burnout-Proof Study Tracker, PhD Roadmap and the Lumen 7-Step Time Planner. They work offline, and what you enter stays on this device.

For planning only. Not financial, tax, legal, medical, mental-health or career advice. Check loan details with your servicer and StudentAid.gov.
