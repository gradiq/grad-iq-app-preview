Grad-IQ Planners by IQ Learning

Privacy policy

DRAFT, last updated October 17, 2026. IQ Learning must review this text and add its contact details before the app is submitted to a store.

The short version

Grad-IQ Planners does not collect, send or sell any of your data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Everything you type stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your planner entries (for example budgets, loan details, study logs, milestones and weekly plans) are saved only on your device, inside the app's private storage.
  • Nothing is sent to IQ Learning or anyone else. The app makes no network requests and works offline.

When data leaves the app

  • Only when you choose to. If you tap a Backup or CSV button, the app makes a file and opens your device's share sheet. You pick where it goes (for example Files, Drive or email). That transfer is handled by the app or service you pick, under its own privacy policy.
  • Tapping a source link (for example StudentAid.gov) opens that website in your browser. That website's own privacy policy applies.

Deleting your data

Use Clear all inside a planner to erase that planner's entries, or delete the app to erase everything. IQ Learning holds no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

The app is made for college and graduate students. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
